Hey, I'm JANIYA

Positivity and self-improvement is my everyday goal, but it's not easy. I try new things to see what works and like to share what I've found.

I am a college senior who majors in Public Health and Communications. I serve as the President of Klosure Dance Team and the Vice President of Flow Dance Organization. I am a Peace Corp Prep candidate and I am from Buffalo, New York.

I have been love with dance and visual art since I was 4 and have just recently found out I love leadership and being busy. I love learning new things about myself and the world around me.
